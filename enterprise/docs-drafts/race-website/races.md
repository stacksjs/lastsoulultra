# Race information

## Understand the format

Open [Format](https://lsu.stacksjs.com/format) for the loop structure, start procedure and rules for remaining in the race. Read the specific event's published information before entering. A coaching subscription does not register you for a race.

## Browse the series

**World Series** lists LSU's own races. Choose **Request a race entry** in the header to open the account and application flow. An event's entry window comes from the race service; an advertised date alone does not establish that the form is open.

**Soul Races** are independent backyard ultras listed by LSU. **View race and entry details** opens the organizer's own registration page. Check its venue, date, requirements, fees and terms there.

The series final can be qualification-only rather than accepting ordinary entry requests. Read the status shown for the selected event.

## Request a place

Use your race-entry account in **Profile** or the entry dialog. This is separate from your LSU training login. Create or verify that race account if required, select an open event and complete the requested application information.

An application is a request to be considered. It is different from selection and from a confirmed race place. Use the race service's confirmation to establish your status.

## Confirm a selected place

<RaceConfirmation />

If LSU selects your application, open **Profile** and inspect the entry and its deadline. The confirmation flow has three tasks, which can be completed in any order:

1. Sign the current participant agreement.
2. Save the required address, phone, clothing sizes and emergency-contact details.
3. Pay the entry fee through the checkout offered by the service.

The service confirms the place when all required tasks are complete. If a new agreement version is required, a signature on an older version does not satisfy that task. After checkout, return to Profile and wait for the service to report the payment; returning from a payment page alone does not prove payment succeeded.

For example, signing the agreement and saving details completes two tasks. If payment is still pending, the place is not yet fully confirmed. Check the status before paying again or sending a duplicate application.

Deadlines in your Profile are displayed in your device's local time. World Series entry-window dates use the event's time zone. Confirm the zone and the event's instructions when planning around a deadline.

## Results and profiles

Open **Results overview** in the footer, then the season's results. Linked public runner profiles show published race information. Those results are separate from private training progress and health data.

The live board mirrors the organizer's published feed. If it cannot refresh, read its stale or unavailable status rather than treating old standings as current. Historical season results remain separate from the live snapshot.

## Questions for the organizer

Use [Contact & FAQ](https://lsu.stacksjs.com/contact) and read the published FAQ before writing. Training Messages does not automatically reach the race organizer. Avoid submitting test applications or payments to the real race service.
