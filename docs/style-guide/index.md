## Review artwork in context

The illustration catalog contains 178 movement figures covering all 184 built-in exercises. Every exercise links to its matching figure. Entries identify side, front or overhead views; overhead figures are shown against a mat or bench outline. A shared figure illustrates a movement pattern; it does not replace a session's technique instructions or prescribe that exercise to every runner.

Open a figure to compare its sampled poses on light and dark surfaces. Expand **Play animation** to view the moving asset. Keep reduced motion enabled if you prefer a still presentation.

## Leave useful feedback

Share the page link, movement or typeface name, browser and screen size. For an exercise figure, describe the pose or equipment contact that needs attention. For type, note the text and size that was difficult to read. Keep account passwords and personal training data out of screenshots.

## Web and native training

The training site and the iPhone app are the same training application, drawn in LSU's colors and typefaces. Workout detail, exercise demonstrations, feedback controls and account actions match between them. The navigation differs: on the website a phone keeps the primary destinations at the bottom and puts the rest above the content, while wider screens move navigation to the side. The iPhone app has five tabs along the bottom, a few destinations at the top of the screen and a Messages bubble beside the tabs. See [Getting started](/getting-started#find-your-way-around) for the names in English and German. Browser audio uploads and native recording use the same review-before-send workflow.

## Staff and coach surfaces

The [admin](https://training.lsu.stacksjs.com/staff/experience) and [coach](https://training.lsu.stacksjs.com/coaches/methods) workspaces share the app's controls, form behavior, lists and grouped layouts. They use the published LSU palette and fonts: black page background, `#0d0d0f` panels, `#333338` separators, red `#dd0f15` actions, Anton headings and Inter body text for the training app. This guide uses Barlow for reading text. Inputs and actions have at least 44 px touch targets. The workspace menu collapses on phones. A link below each workspace leads back to the protected [demo walkthrough](/demo).
