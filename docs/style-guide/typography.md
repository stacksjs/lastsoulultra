# Typography

The app uses Anton for headings and Inter for body text. This guide uses Barlow for extended reading and Share Tech Mono for structured details. The samples load the supplied self-hosted fonts.

## Anton: display headings

Anton Regular, weight 400, is the condensed face used for prominent app and training headings. Use it for short titles with enough space around them.

## Inter: app reading and interface text

Inter is the app's body face, paired with Anton headings. The variable font supports weight changes without changing families. Use readable sizes, clear labels and comfortable line spacing for training instructions and error recovery.

<div class="lsu-guide"><div class="sg-specimen" style="font-family:Inter,system-ui,sans-serif"><p>Review the session. Record what you actually did, then tell your coach how it felt.</p><p>ABCDEFGHIJKLMNOPQRSTUVWXYZ<br>abcdefghijklmnopqrstuvwxyz<br>0123456789 · Ä Ö Ü ä ö ü ß</p></div></div>

## Barlow: reading and interface text

Barlow is the documentation reading face. The app uses Inter for instructions and interface copy. The supplied family includes weights 300, 400, 500, 600 and 700, plus Regular Italic. Choose weight to establish hierarchy; keep longer instructions at a comfortable reading size.

## Share Tech Mono: labels and structured details

Share Tech Mono Regular, weight 400, is used for compact labels, times and event details. Give labels room to wrap on a narrow screen. Use the body face for long instructions.

## Check readability

Review the specimens on a phone and at desktop width. Zoom the page and confirm that labels, digits and headings remain visible. Use the [color specimens](/style-guide/colors-and-interface) to assess text on its intended background. The [workout figures](/style-guide/illustrations) follow the device's reduced-motion preference independently of these static type samples.
