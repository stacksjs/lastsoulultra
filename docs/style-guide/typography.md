# Typography

LSU uses four typefaces, each with a distinct job. The samples below load the same self-hosted font files as the site. Body copy remains readable alongside the condensed display headings and numeric race clock.

## Anton: display headings

Anton Regular, weight 400, is the condensed face used for prominent race and training headings. Use it for short titles with enough space around them.

## Barlow: reading and interface text

Barlow carries instructions, descriptions and most interface copy. The supplied family includes weights 300, 400, 500, 600 and 700, plus Regular Italic. Choose weight to establish hierarchy; keep longer instructions at a comfortable reading size.

## Share Tech Mono: labels and structured details

Share Tech Mono Regular, weight 400, is used for compact labels, times and event details. Give labels room to wrap on a narrow screen. Use the body face for long instructions.

<div class="lsu-guide"><div class="sg-specimen"><p class="sg-label">SHARE TECH MONO · REGULAR 400</p><p class="sg-mono">SESSION 04 · 06:30 · 8.5 KM</p><p class="sg-mono sg-glyphs">ABCDEFGHIJKLMNOPQRSTUVWXYZ<br>abcdefghijklmnopqrstuvwxyz<br>0123456789 : / + -</p></div></div>

## DSEG7: race-clock digits

DSEG7 Classic Bold, weight 700, supplies the seven-segment race display. It is a numeric accent, not a face for paragraphs or ordinary navigation. These are static digit specimens, not a running countdown.

## Check readability

Review the specimens on a phone and at desktop width. Zoom the page and confirm that labels, digits and headings remain visible. Use the [color specimens](/style-guide/colors-and-interface) to assess text on its intended background. The [workout figures](/style-guide/illustrations) follow the device's reduced-motion preference independently of these static type samples.
