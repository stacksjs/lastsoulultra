# Coach guide

## Enter the coach workspace

Sign in with a coach account. Choose the coach workspace from the account menu if it is not already active. A coach's access is separate from organization administration; method authors do not automatically manage every LSU setting.

## Maintain a method

Open the coaching methods area. Give the method a clear name and a useful public summary. Keep the private instructions precise about training structure, permitted adaptations and the information needed from a runner. Draft changes before publishing.

A method is the author's approach, not a promise that every runner receives the same week. Do not present demonstration instructions as an approved LSU coach's method.

## Prepare training and offers

Create or review the training plan that supplies sessions. Organization managers connect the eligible plan, product and published method into an offer. Price, currency, billing interval and included services belong to that offer. See [Administration](/administration).

## Review subscriber training

Use the permitted subscriber training view. A self-coaching subscription does not automatically create a one-to-one coach assignment. Access is checked against your organization membership, role and the method or subscriber concerned.

## Verify publication

Check the published offer as a visitor, then sign in as a subscriber and confirm that the expected method and sessions appear. Keep private methodology out of the public summary.
