# Troubleshooting

## The browser keeps asking for a password

Use the shared preview username and password supplied with the handoff. Your personal training password does not unlock this browser dialog. The same preview credentials protect the race site, this guide and its assets. The training site at training.lsu.stacksjs.com and the iPhone app never show this dialog.

## I can open the site but cannot see training

Open Coaching and sign into the intended training account. A visitor has no subscriber sessions. Try the supplied runner account for populated workouts and progress. Refresh the screen after switching accounts.

## Signing up offers Continue with HQ

The email address you typed belongs to an HQ account. Choose **Continue with HQ** to use that account, or create a separate Last Soul Ultra account with its own password. Both work; the first brings your HQ profile and, if you approve it, your training history.

## Continue with HQ or Sign in with Apple does not work

Continue with HQ links only to an HQ account whose email address is verified. Verify it in HQ first, then try again, and approve the sharing screen HQ shows you. Sign in with Apple is offered in the iPhone app, not on the website. Google sign-in is not set up for Last Soul Ultra. Email and password always work.

## The welcome tour opened

It opens once, on a new account's first sign-in. Choose **Skip** to leave it. **Settings** → **Take the tour again** brings it back.

## I cannot enter administration

Sign in as the owner or one of the supplied administrators, then switch into the Last Soul Ultra organization workspace. In the iPhone app, open **Me** → **Manage Last Soul Ultra**. An athlete or coach account does not automatically have administrator rights. If the role was just changed, sign out and back in.

An administrator added through the deployment settings has no usable password at first. Use **Continue with HQ** to sign in.

## A page says an offer or section is unavailable

LSU may not have published it yet, or the method or product behind it may no longer be available. An administrator should open the published LSU app settings (the brand name, logo, navigation and the offers the app shows) and check the method, its author, the training plan and the product it points at.

## Voice recording or transcription fails

Use HTTPS, allow the microphone for the page and use a supported browser. Check that the device has an input available. If a transcript cannot be produced, type feedback instead. Do not repeatedly resend a message without checking whether it already arrived.

## Feedback sends but no reply arrives

The written reply and any plan change come from AI on the coach's own key. If the coach has not added one, the feedback screen says so before you write, and the coach's **Training methods** page asks them to add it under **Settings → AI Keys**. Transcription does not depend on it. If your message appears but no reply follows after a minute or two, refresh once, then report it with the time; do not send the same feedback again.

## Billing actions are unavailable for a demo

The seeded access is a demonstration without a real payment-provider subscription. Browse its status and training. Do not use a real card or attempt a refund against sample records.

## What to include in a report

Record the page, account role, action, expected result and visible error. Include the time and time zone. Do not include passwords, session tokens, personal health information or card data. Use the [Contact page](https://lsu.stacksjs.com/contact) for the organiser's published support route.
