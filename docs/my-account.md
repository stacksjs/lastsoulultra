# Your account and access

## Two sign-ins in the preview

The browser's preview password unlocks the private website. Your app email and password identify your personal training account. Passing the preview gate does not sign you into an athlete, coach or administrator account.

## Your profile

Open the [account panel](https://lsu.stacksjs.com/coaching/account) on the website to review your subscription and the fitness-sharing preference offered for an active enrollment. This screen does not contain every profile setting. Use the full account workspace for profile settings where available. Calendar dates follow the time zone stored on your account.

In the iOS app, the **Me** tab is your profile, notifications, settings and LSU's privacy policy. Your subscription is one row down: choose **Your subscription** there, which opens the same status, renewal and billing actions as the website panel. In German that row reads **Dein Abo**.

## Switch roles safely

Use the account menu's sign-out action where available, then sign in with the next demo account and reload open training tabs. For a clean review of the embedded panels, use a separate browser profile, or close all private windows before starting another private session. Private windows in the same browser can share a session.

A runner should see their own training, a coach should see their permitted workspace, and an administrator should enter the LSU organization workspace.

## Subscription and billing

Use the [account panel](https://lsu.stacksjs.com/coaching/account) to review renewal and billing actions offered for your subscription. Read [Plans and subscriptions](/training/plans) before changing renewal.

## Passwords and support

Keep the shared preview password and your personal password separate. Demo addresses ending in `.test` do not receive email, so do not use email recovery as your only way back into a demo account. Find the current shared demo passwords in the protected [demo walkthrough](/demo#demo-credentials).
