# Your account and access

## Two sign-ins in the preview

The browser's preview password unlocks the private website. Your app email and password identify your personal training account. Passing the preview gate does not sign you into an athlete, coach or administrator account.

## Your profile

Open **Me** or the account panel to review your account. Keep your time zone accurate so workouts are shown on the intended calendar date. Your role and active organization determine which management screens are available.

## Switch roles safely

Sign out, then sign in with the next demo account. Reload any other open training tabs. A runner account should show its own training; a coach account should show the coach's permitted work; an administrator should enter the LSU organization workspace.

## Subscription and billing

Use the [account panel](https://lsu.stacksjs.com/coaching/account) to review renewal and billing actions offered for your subscription. Read [Plans and subscriptions](/training/plans) before changing renewal.

## Passwords and support

Keep the shared preview password and your personal password separate. Demo addresses ending in `.test` do not receive email, so do not use email recovery as your only way back into a demo account. Ask the person who supplied the credentials for a replacement if needed.
