# Demo walkthrough

This walkthrough and all demo credentials are protected by the same preview login as the website. All seeded training and conversations are fictional demonstration content.

The [roles page](/roles) maps all twelve accounts and explains organization permissions versus training entitlements.

## Demo credentials

Use [Training](https://lsu.stacksjs.com/coaching/calendar) for athlete and browsing accounts, [Administration](https://lsu.stacksjs.com/admin) for Sam and Pawel, and the [Coach workspace](https://lsu.stacksjs.com/coach) for the coach. The race-entry Profile has a separate account system.

These are shared review accounts. Their `.test` addresses do not receive email. Use the listed passwords rather than email recovery, and keep real personal or payment information out of them. Complete your own policy choices when a workspace asks.

## 1. Check the preview gate

Open the site in a private browser window. Confirm that it asks for the shared preview credentials. Open this guide in that same window and confirm it uses the same gate.

## 2. Browse as a visitor

Sign in with the visitor account. Open Coaching and inspect the published offer. Confirm that private subscriber workouts are not available to a visitor without an enrollment.

## 3. Review an enrolled athlete

Use Alex or Jamie. Both athlete accounts have the €29.99/month, tax included, Run + Strength tier with complimentary review access. Open Workouts, inspect a completed session and a future session, then check Progress and the method Library. Each enrolled athlete starts with 48 sessions: 32 running and 16 strength, with 21 sessions completed at setup. Each also has 21 days of sample health metrics. The sample dates include recent history and upcoming sessions. Counts can change as reviewers complete or edit training.

Open a **Strength A** or **Strength B** session. Inspect its illustrated exercises, blocks and sets, and try ticking off a set. Compare it with a running session and its timed steps. The two disciplines belong to the same plan.

Open **Say how it felt**, select the combined method, and send a fictional training note. Wait for its AI response. Then record a short voice note or upload an audio file, transcribe it, review and correct the text, and send it. Both enrolled demo athletes can use these flows. See [Feedback](/training/feedback).

Open Health and Messages to inspect any supplied sample records. Avoid connecting real devices or entering personal information.

Open the [Style Guide](/style-guide/) to review the typefaces, colors and interface specimens alongside the workout figures.

## 4. Review the coach

Use a fresh session and the coach account. Open the [coach workspace](https://lsu.stacksjs.com/coach) and inspect the sample method and plan. Then open the [exercise library](/training/exercise-library) and [illustration catalog](/style-guide/illustrations). The demo plans do not prescribe every exercise in the reference. Keep demonstration methodology clearly identified as sample content.

## 5. Review administration

Open [Administration](https://lsu.stacksjs.com/admin) with Sam's or Pawel's administrator account. Complete your own policy choices if prompted. Administration opens the LSU Experience directly; verify that its settings and linked offers load. Use the workspace menu to reach the other management tools. Confirm that an athlete account cannot perform the same management action.

Explore the [data model and ERD](/erd) to trace account permissions, subscriptions, workouts and feedback.

## 6. Do not test payments

Sample training access is not a paid order and carries no real payment-provider subscription. The review accounts already hold the tier with complimentary access, so there is nothing to buy in order to review training.

Do not enter a card at all, your own or a test one, and do not try to bill, refund or reverse a charge against a seeded record. If you need to walk the purchase flow itself, ask whoever sent you this handoff to confirm which payment mode the preview is running in and to set up a throwaway account for it.

## Review notes

To switch roles, use sign-out where available or start a clean browser session as described in [Your account](/my-account#switch-roles-safely). Separate private windows can share a login until all private windows are closed.

Record the account role, page and observed behavior. Send feedback to the person who supplied the handoff. Keep passwords out of screenshots and shared issue reports.

## Admin and coach workspaces

[Administration](https://lsu.stacksjs.com/admin) opens LSU’s Experience settings directly for Sam and Pawel. [Coach workspace](https://lsu.stacksjs.com/coach) opens methods, with [Exercises](https://lsu.stacksjs.com/coach/exercises) and [Subscribers](https://lsu.stacksjs.com/coach/subscribers) in the workspace navigation. Both workspaces use the native app’s controls and grouped layouts, with LSU’s black surfaces, red actions, Anton headings and Barlow text. On a phone, open **Workspace menu** to reach the other tools and sign out. Links and saved changes stay in the LSU organization. The website password also protects these entry pages.
