# Privacy and account boundaries

## Public and private information

Race information, results and published offers are meant to be browsed. Your training, health metrics, messages and billing actions require the appropriate personal account. The private preview adds a browser password in front of the race site and this guide.

Training data lives in Last Soul Ultra's own installation, stored in Germany. It has its own accounts, database and uploads, separate from the race entry service and from HQ.

## Organization access

An LSU administrator can manage the LSU organization within the permissions granted to that role. Organization administration does not grant unrestricted access to every person's private health information or to another organization.

## Feedback and recordings

Review the transcript before sending voice feedback. The adaptive training conversation uses AI inside the method's rules; it does not imply that a human coach listens to every recording. Avoid adding information that is unrelated to training.

## Linked HQ accounts and connected devices

Signing in with **Continue with HQ** shares only the parts of your HQ profile you choose, and health data only if you tick it. Last Soul Ultra keeps a copy as part of your account here. Devices such as Strava, Oura and WHOOP connect through HQ's registered apps; HQ holds the authorization but does not store your health data. See [Your account](/my-account#signing-in-with-an-hq-account) and [Health](/training/health#connect-a-device-or-service).

## Shared demonstration accounts

Demo users and their training are fictional. Anyone with the same demo credentials can see that account's data, so do not add real health information, personal messages, payment details or wearable connections.

The training app has its own [privacy policy](https://training.lsu.stacksjs.com/legal/lsu/privacy) and [terms](https://training.lsu.stacksjs.com/legal/lsu/terms); these are the documents you agree to on first sign-in. The race site's [Privacy](https://lsu.stacksjs.com/privacy), [Terms](https://lsu.stacksjs.com/terms) and [Imprint](https://lsu.stacksjs.com/imprint) (Impressum) cover the race site itself.
