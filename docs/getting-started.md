# Getting started

## Enter the private preview

Open [Last Soul Ultra](https://lsu.stacksjs.com). Your browser first asks for the shared preview username and password. This protects the entire site, including this guide. It is separate from your personal app account.

Open **Coaching**, then **Open your training**. Sign in with your personal account when prompted. For a demonstration, use the account supplied for the role you want to review. See the [demo walkthrough](/demo).

## Find your way around

The race site includes Format, World Series, Soul Races, Results, Coaching and Contact. Coaching links lead to the training tiers, workouts, feedback, progress, method library, health, messages and account panels.

The training app's main tabs are **Store**, **Workouts**, **Health**, **Messages** and **Me**. Progress and method details are available from the training panels. Some modules appear only after publication or an active enrollment.

## Check your account

Confirm your name and time zone before reviewing scheduled sessions. Calendar dates follow your account's time zone. A session around midnight can fall on a different date for someone in another zone.

Use your own account for private training and messages. Sign out before trying a different demo role, then refresh the training panel.

## If a panel is empty

Check that you signed into the intended account, that its subscription grants access and that the module has been published. A visitor without a subscription can browse offers but does not receive a subscriber's workouts. See [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting).
