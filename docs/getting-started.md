# Getting started

## Choose the right sign-in

Use one **LSU training account** across the [web app](https://training.lsu.stacksjs.com) and installed native apps. Each device has its own signed-in session, while your profile, training and permitted workspaces belong to the same account. The [Visual guide](/visual-guide#one-account-across-the-apps) shows this relationship.

This review guide may require a shared preview username and password. That gate opens the guide; it does not sign you into a personal training account. An embedded app also authenticates with LSU directly. See [Web-component integration](/integration) for the host/application boundary.

## Sign into training

Open [Training](https://training.lsu.stacksjs.com/login). Use your **LSU account** email address and password, then follow any additional verification the account requires. The supplied review accounts use the credentials in the protected [demo walkthrough](/demo#demo-credentials).

This LSU installation disables **Continue with HQ**. An existing HQ account does not sign you into LSU or automatically copy your training history. Use the sign-in options actually shown for LSU. Social sign-in availability depends on the installation's configured providers.

To create your own account, choose the sign-up action from the login screen. Use an email address you can receive mail at. Shared demo addresses ending in `.test` cannot receive recovery emails.

The iPhone app is distributed through TestFlight to invited testers. Use the invitation supplied with your preview access, then sign into the same LSU training account. Web and iPhone sessions are separate, but read the same account data.

## Complete the first sign-in

Read and agree to the LSU terms, privacy policy and Health & Safety information when prompted. Complete your own choices rather than copying another reviewer's consent.

The [onboarding guide](/onboarding) explains each screen, why it appears and what setup changes. The welcome tour introduces the main screens and offers setup and connection options. Choose **Skip** to go to the app, or revisit it from **Settings** under **Take the tour again** (**Tour erneut starten** in German).

## Find your way around

The training app has its own navigation. The published LSU navigation currently leads with **Store**, **Workouts**, **Health**, **Messages** and **Me**. Enabled sections and wording can change when LSU updates its Experience.

In the current LSU iPhone build, the five bottom tabs are **Today**, **Week**, **Exercises**, **Backyard** and **Me**. **Health** is a top-bar shortcut, while **Progress** and **Workouts** are in the More menu. Store and Calendar are reached from the top controls. The Messages bubble shows unread conversations. The layout comes from LSU's product configuration, so it differs from the general HQ app.

| You want to | Where to go |
| --- | --- |
| See today's session | **Today** in the app |
| Browse planned dates | **Calendar**; [Workouts](/training/workouts) explains the schedule |
| Find a subscription | **Me** → **Coaching accounts**, then choose it |
| Send feedback about that method | **Training feedback** on the subscription screen, when enabled |
| Compare assigned and finished sessions | **Training progress** on the subscription screen |
| Chart all recorded training | **More** → **Progress** in the LSU app |
| Change profile details or time zone | **Me** or **Settings** |

The general AI assistant and a subscription's feedback conversation serve different purposes. Use the subscription's feedback screen when giving the weekly feedback its next training block requires.

## Your first training week

1. Check your profile, units and time zone. Dates follow your account's time zone.
2. Open your subscription and read what the package includes.
3. Open a planned session, read its instructions and record what you actually do.
4. When the weekly feedback prompt opens, describe how the week went and any availability changes.
5. Wait for LSU's coach review before expecting the next block on your calendar.

The current catalog releases seven days at a time. A twelve-week package does not put twelve approved weeks on your calendar at once. The older fictional demo plan has a fuller calendar so reviewers can inspect past and future training. See [Plans and subscriptions](/training/plans).

If a screen is empty, first check the account, enrollment and publication status. Then use [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting).
