# Progress and the method library

## Review completed training

Open [Progress](https://lsu.stacksjs.com/coaching/progress). Compare assigned sessions with completed sessions across the training you currently have access to. Totals can include older sessions outside the calendar's visible range.

A completion total describes recorded sessions. It is not a race result, a fitness diagnosis or a guarantee of readiness. If a completed session is missing, reopen it in Workouts and check its saved status and enrollment.

## Read your method

Open [The method](https://lsu.stacksjs.com/coaching/library). Review the author and the published summary of the training approach for your tier. A published summary explains the approach; the author's private methodology is not exposed here.

## More than one tier

Check the subscription or method attached to the training you are reviewing. Offers, methods and enrollments are related but distinct: an offer describes what you can join, a method defines the approach, and an enrollment grants your sessions.
