# Plans and subscriptions

## Browse published tiers

Open **Coaching** or **Store** to see published offers. Read the method summary, author, included training, price, currency and billing interval. A tier can cover running alone or combine other training disciplines; only the published offer establishes what is included.

## The $29 Run + Strength review tier

The private preview includes **Running and strength, demo**, priced at **USD 29 per month**. It includes both running and strength in one enrollment. Both Alex and Jamie use this same tier; their historical runner email names do not restrict them to running.

The fictional eight-week plan has four running sessions and two structured strength sessions per week, with exercise illustrations, sets and repetitions or timed efforts. Open a strength session to review its blocks and set controls. This sample illustrates the app and is not an approved LSU training prescription. Personal one-to-one coaching and Mind training are not included in this demo offer.

The review accounts have complimentary access. The displayed price describes the sample tier; it is not an active charge. See [Roles and plan access](/roles) for the complete account map.

## Join a tier

Choose the offer and follow its checkout instructions. Confirm the total and recurring terms before authorizing payment. Return to the app after checkout and wait for the confirmed subscription or purchase to appear. A browser return by itself is not proof that payment succeeded.

An active enrollment supplies the sessions shown in **Workouts**, the method in **Library** and its totals in **Progress**. This is self-coaching within an authored method unless an offer explicitly says otherwise.

## Renewal and access

Open **Me** or **Account** to inspect subscription status. Where available, cancel renewal to stop the next renewal, or resume it before cancellation takes effect. Read the date and status shown: stopping renewal and losing access immediately are different events.

Payment problems can affect access. Use the billing action offered by your account to correct a payment method, and refresh after the provider confirms the change.

## Demonstration accounts

The supplied subscriber already has sample training access. Its seeded subscription has no payment-provider charge attached. Do not enter a real card or attempt to bill, refund or test a payment against these demonstration records. Browse the sample offer and review the existing access instead.
