# Feedback and plan adaptation

## Choose the right training context

Open [Say how it felt](https://lsu.stacksjs.com/coaching/feedback). Select the tier or subscription you are discussing when more than one is available.

## Send text feedback

Describe the session and the reason for an adjustment: how the effort felt, whether recovery was difficult, or which days are no longer available. Review your message before sending it. Keep it specific enough for the method to distinguish a missed workout from a changed weekly schedule.

## Record voice feedback

Choose **Record voice feedback** and allow microphone access when the browser asks. Stop recording, then select **Transcribe recording**. Wait for the transcript, read and correct it, then select **Send feedback**. Recording, transcription and sending are separate actions. You can also upload an audio file where the upload control is available.

The enrolled Alex and Jamie demo accounts support both AI feedback and voice transcription. Other supplied accounts do not gain method feedback just from their staff role.

Raw audio is processed temporarily and removed after transcription. The private transcript receipt expires after 24 hours; text you choose to send becomes part of the feedback conversation.

If recording is unavailable, use text feedback. A denied microphone permission, unsupported browser or unavailable transcription service can prevent the voice path without preventing text entry.

## Review the response

AI interprets feedback inside the coach's method. This is not a private session with a human coach, and a voice message does not imply that someone listened to the recording. Where a proposed change needs approval, inspect it before confirming. Check the resulting future sessions in [Workouts](/training/workouts).

## When something fails

Keep the text you intended to send. Check whether a reply or proposal already appeared before retrying. If transcription reports that the service is busy, keep the recording and retry; a successful upload reuses its transcript rather than transcribing twice. Service availability can affect transcription and adaptation; see [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting).
