# Workouts and calendar

## Open your sessions

Open [Workouts](https://lsu.stacksjs.com/coaching/calendar). Sign in as a subscriber. Select a session to inspect its title, sport, date, planned duration, distance and instructions. Read any warm-up, main effort and cool-down steps before recording a result.

Only sessions from the plan you are signed up to appear in the LSU app. An account with no plan can have an empty calendar.

## Exercise demonstrations

For strength exercises and drills, consult the [exercise library](/training/exercise-library) for the built-in names, equipment and matching illustrations. The [illustration catalog](/style-guide/illustrations) lists all supplied SVG figures and their sampled poses. All 184 built-in exercises have a matching figure. Exercises with the same movement can share a figure. Open Strength A or Strength B in the demo calendar to inspect its exercise figures, instructions, blocks and sets. Tick a set to record it, then reopen the session to check the saved state. Follow the session's own instructions and demonstration when provided.

## Record what happened

Open a session and use the available completion controls. Enter the actual result rather than copying the plan when the two differ. Where offered, record duration, distance, effort and how you felt, then save. Reopen the session to check the saved result.

Use the notes or [feedback panel](/training/feedback) to explain missed or changed training. A calendar entry by itself does not tell the method why a session changed.

## Browse dates

Move through the displayed calendar range or load more sessions when offered. Dates use your account's time zone. [Progress](/training/progress) counts training across every plan you are signed up to, and can include completed sessions outside the calendar's current window.

The iOS app keeps your coming sessions and their exercise videos on the phone, so **Workouts** still opens where there is no signal and sends what you recorded once you are back online. If the app closes during a session, it reopens that session where the clock had reached. Where notifications are on, the app refreshes your calendar when your plan is synced. Turning on airplane mode and opening Workouts is the quickest way to check this.

## Changes to a plan

Adaptive coaching can revise eligible future unfinished sessions. Completed history is a record of what you did and does not change. A revision is applied straight away, so read the explanation on the feedback reply and use **Undo this change** there if you disagree.
