# Workouts and calendar

## Open your sessions

Open [Workouts](https://lsu.stacksjs.com/coaching/calendar). Sign in as a subscriber. Select a session to inspect its title, sport, date, planned duration, distance and instructions. Read any warm-up, main effort and cool-down steps before recording a result.

Only sessions from your accessible training enrollment appear in the branded training panel. An account with no enrollment can have an empty calendar.

## Exercise demonstrations

For strength exercises and drills, consult the [exercise library](/training/exercise-library) for the built-in names, equipment and available illustration mappings. The [illustration catalog](/style-guide/illustrations) lists all supplied SVG figures and their sampled poses. Several exercises share a movement figure, and some entries currently have no mapped illustration. Follow the session's own instructions and demonstration when provided.

## Record what happened

Open a session and use the available completion controls. Enter the actual result rather than copying the plan when the two differ. Where offered, record duration, distance, effort and how you felt, then save. Reopen the session to check the saved result.

Use the notes or [feedback panel](/training/feedback) to explain missed or changed training. A calendar entry by itself does not tell the method why a session changed.

## Browse dates

Move through the displayed calendar range or load more sessions when offered. Dates use your account's time zone. [Progress](/training/progress) counts training across accessible enrollments and can include completed sessions outside the calendar's current window.

## Changes to a plan

Adaptive coaching can revise eligible future unfinished sessions. Completed history is a record of what you did. Review any proposed changes and their explanation before accepting them when approval is requested.
