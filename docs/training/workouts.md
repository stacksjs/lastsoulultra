# Workouts and calendar

## Open your sessions

Open [Workouts](https://lsu.stacksjs.com/coaching/calendar) on the website. In the iPhone app, **Today** shows the day's session and **Calendar**, at the top left, shows the weeks around it. The **Workouts** tab is where you record a workout of your own and keep saved workouts. Sign in as a subscriber. Select a session to inspect its title, sport, date, planned duration, distance and instructions. Read any warm-up, main effort and cool-down steps before recording a result.

The calendar shows the training on your account: sessions from the plans you are signed up to, workouts you record or import. An account with no plan and no recorded training has an empty calendar.

## Exercise demonstrations

For strength exercises and drills, consult the [exercise library](/training/exercise-library) for the built-in names, equipment and matching illustrations. The [illustration catalog](/style-guide/illustrations) lists all supplied SVG figures and their sampled poses. All 184 built-in exercises have a matching figure. Exercises with the same movement can share a figure. Open Strength A or Strength B in the demo calendar to inspect its exercise figures, instructions, blocks and sets. Tick a set to record it, then reopen the session to check the saved state. Follow the session's own instructions and demonstration when provided.

## Record what happened

Open a session and use the available completion controls. Enter the actual result rather than copying the plan when the two differ. Where offered, record duration, distance, effort and how you felt, then save. Reopen the session to check the saved result.

Use the notes or [feedback screen](/training/feedback) to explain missed or changed training. A calendar entry by itself does not tell the coach why a session changed.

For example, if you stop a planned 90-minute run after 65 minutes, record 65 minutes as the actual duration. Describe the reason in feedback. Marking the planned session complete without the actual result can give the reviewer a misleading picture.

Ticking one strength set records that set; check the session's own completion status as well. A checked set and a completed workout are different records.

## Browse dates

Move through the displayed calendar range or load more sessions when offered. Dates use your account's time zone. [Progress](/training/progress) counts training across every plan you are signed up to, and can include completed sessions outside the calendar's current window.

The iPhone app keeps your coming sessions and their exercise videos on the phone, so **Today** and **Calendar** still open where there is no signal, and the app sends what you recorded once you are back online. If the app closes during a session, it reopens that session where the clock had reached. Where notifications are on, the app refreshes your calendar when your plan is synced. Turning on airplane mode and opening Calendar is the quickest way to check this.

## Changes to a plan

The current catalog supplies training one approved seven-day block at a time. A future week can be absent while feedback is due, a draft is being prepared or an LSU coach is reviewing it. Read the status shown before treating an empty week as missing data.

Send method feedback during the window shown for your subscription. Coaches review and assign future training; the general AI assistant is configured for insights and does not automatically rewrite sessions. Completed sessions remain the record of what you did. See [Feedback and weekly coach review](/training/feedback).

The fictional Alex and Jamie demo calendars were seeded with eight weeks at once so reviewers could inspect history and upcoming sessions. Their populated future dates do not describe how newly purchased catalog packages are released.
