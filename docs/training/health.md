# Health and connected data

## Open your health panel

Open [Health](https://lsu.stacksjs.com/coaching/health) while signed in. The panel reads the health information available to your own account, such as sleep and heart-rate summaries. The metrics shown depend on connected sources, sync status and your permissions.

An empty panel can mean no source is connected or that the source has not supplied data for the selected period. It does not mean that a zero-valued measurement was recorded.

## Data sharing

Your health data belongs to your personal account. Browsing LSU or buying a tier does not automatically give the race organiser access to all of your private metrics. Review any explicit fitness-sharing preference before enabling it.

## Sample data

Demo metrics are fictional examples for reviewing the interface. They are not imported from a real person's wearable and should not be used to make training decisions.

## Check missing information

Confirm the signed-in account, source connection and most recent sync. Avoid connecting a real wearable to a shared demo account. See [Privacy](/privacy) for the boundary between account data and published information.
