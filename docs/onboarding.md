# Onboarding, step by step

Onboarding helps you enter the right account, understand the training screens and make your own setup choices. It does not apply for a race, buy coaching or release an approved training week.

There are two parts: the required policy agreement, then a welcome tour and setup. The same tour engine powers the website and iPhone app. Layout and controls differ by device; the account's saved progress is shared.

## Before the tour

### 1. LSU start

Choose **Get started** or compare plans. Change language if needed.

Explain LSU's coaching before asking you to create an account. Next: account creation or the Store.

### 2. Create an account

Enter your own details and an email address you can receive mail at. Review the sign-in options actually offered.

Establish your LSU identity. This installation disables HQ sign-in. Next: verification or sign-in, as required by the account.

### 3. Sign in

Use the LSU account's credentials. Follow additional verification if prompted.

Keep personal training separate from the shared preview gate. Next: required policy agreement, a due tour or the account's home.

### 4. Email verification

Follow the verification link sent to your address.

Confirm access to that mailbox. Next: return to the sign-in or account flow. Shared `.test` demo addresses cannot receive these messages.

### 5. Forgot/reset password

Request recovery for your own reachable mailbox, then use its reset link.

Recover access without exposing the old password. Next: sign in with the changed password. Use the supplied demo password for shared review accounts.

### 6. Policy agreement

Read the linked terms, privacy and Health & Safety information. Make your own choices and choose **I agree** to continue.

Record acceptance of the current policy versions. A later policy update can require a new answer. Next: the welcome tour when due, otherwise the appropriate home. Choosing **I don't agree** signs out.

The policy agreement can appear as a website dialog or as a dedicated screen. The iPhone screen has no tabs or swipe-back exit while an answer is required. You can read the policies before deciding. This agreement is separate from the tour's training-safety question and any feature-specific agreement, such as breathwork.

## The welcome and feature screens

The first page introduces the account's main work. The following pages explain one feature at a time, using real LSU screen captures with the relevant control highlighted. Swipe, choose a page dot or use the offered navigation controls to move through the tour. **Back** revisits earlier information. **Skip** leaves the tour and records that choice; it does not silently finish setup.

| Athlete screen | What it explains | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Today | Today's session and the available recovery context. | Start with the work relevant to today. |
| Calendar | Planned and completed sessions across dates. | Distinguish an assigned session from one you recorded. |
| Workout player | Steps, targets, timing and exercise demonstrations. | Read the session before starting and understand what to record. |
| Wearables | Connections that can bring supported workouts or health data into LSU. | Avoid entering the same information twice. Availability depends on device and provider. |
| Progress | Account-wide training trends. | Review recorded training over time; this differs from a subscription's assigned/completed summary. |
| Your coach, when assigned | The route to a coaching conversation. | Keep questions close to their training context. Weekly subscription feedback still belongs on the subscription's feedback screen. |
| Breathwork, when enabled | Relax and its breathing-plan entry point. | Find an optional recovery practice. Its own agreement and setup are separate. |

LSU's coaches set training. The general HQ tour's self-authored starter-plan page is therefore omitted here. Completing LSU's tour does not generate a personal eight-week plan. An empty calendar can be correct until you have an enrollment and an approved block.

## Athlete setup, one question at a time

### 1. Goals

Choose all relevant goals: a race, fitness, strength, weight management or health. Describe what you want from training. Selected goals are added to your profile during setup.

### 2. Main sport

Choose the sport that best describes your training. It provides context and updates your profile's sport; it does not enroll you in a paid package. Available choices can be filtered by the installation.

### 3. Weekly time

Choose the approximate time you can realistically train. Give a sustainable starting point, including other commitments. A later availability change still needs communicating to your coach.

### 4. Experience

Choose beginner, intermediate or advanced. Describe relevant experience rather than the level you hope to reach. This answer is not a performance test or medical assessment.

### 5. About you, optional

Provide age and sex if you want to. This is optional training context. You can leave it unanswered.

### 6. Connect a wearable

Open the connection flow or choose to do it later. Authorize the specific provider yourself. Returning to setup should show a completed connection. You can add devices later.

### 7. Health and safety

Read both statements, turn on both confirmations if you agree, then submit. The app records a timestamp for this tour consent. Do not copy another reviewer's choices.

### 8. Summary

Read what LSU will set up, then choose the offered setup action. Check the intended changes before they are applied. In LSU, coaching packages come from the Store and coaches approve weekly blocks.

### 9. Completion

Review the completed items and any failures, then continue. Partial failure should be visible. Retry unfinished work rather than assuming every item succeeded.

For example, a new runner selects **Train for a race**, **Running**, **3 to 5 hours** and **Beginner**, then postpones connecting a watch. Setup can save the goal and sport without connecting a device or buying a plan. In this coach-led LSU installation, the athlete continues to the Store to choose coaching; a future training block still needs its normal feedback and coach-review workflow.

## Why another account sees another tour

The tour follows capabilities and permissions, not the account name. A coach profile takes priority; an administrator who also coaches sees the coach tour. This is why two people with organization access can see different introductions.

| Tour | Who sees it | Introduction and setup |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Coach | An account with coaching capability. | Roster, calendar, workout builder, plan library, annual plan and Store. Questions ask roster size, sports and coaching style. Setup can update the coach profile and send deliberately entered athlete invitations. Review addresses before submitting. |
| Organization | An owner, administrator or manager with staff capability and no coach profile taking priority. | Business overview, timetable, members, point of sale, communications, shifts and reports. Questions cover classes, membership scale and priorities. Setup can create recurring class starters for selected class types. This is broader organization tooling, not athlete plan enrollment. |
| Staff | Staff capability without one of those management roles and no coach profile taking priority. | The desk or classes, plus the tools their permissions allow. Questions ask work focus and notification preferences. Push is a choice; permitted work email follows the organization's workflow. |
| Athlete | Training capability, without a higher-priority coach/staff context. | Training features, goals, sport, available time, experience, optional personal context, connections and safety. |
| Member | Gym membership capability without training or staff capability. | Classes, billing and Store; questions cover goals, class preferences and visit frequency. Class suggestions and any explicit booking belong to the membership flow. A coaching subscription does not grant this capability. |

Those are the shared platform's possible tours. LSU's configured capabilities determine which are actually relevant. Do not try to unlock a gym workspace by buying coaching. See [Roles and plan access](/roles) and [Administration](/administration).

## Pause, skip, resume and replay

The account saves its answers and current step as the tour advances. If you leave a tour that is still in progress, reopening resumes its saved position. A failed save is reported and retried on a later step; avoid closing immediately after a failure if you need that answer saved.

**Skip** records a skipped tour and leaves. It does not accept unanswered consents, connect a provider, submit invitations or buy anything. You can open the tour again from **Settings** → **Take the tour again**. Replay begins at the first page and retains saved answers. It is not an account reset.

Setup keeps a record of successfully completed tasks. Replaying and pressing setup again avoids repeating tasks already recorded as done; unfinished tasks can be retried. Check the completion screen's actual results, especially after a lost connection. Do not repeatedly invite someone to test the screen.

If the tour cannot load, check your connection and choose **Try again**. If it opens the wrong role, confirm the signed-in account and workspace before changing permissions. If a newly completed tour returns unexpectedly, refresh the account and follow [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting).

## Training level and goal

On the branded iPhone app's first-run path, finishing the tour can open **Training level and goal** before plan selection. This follow-up uses the athlete profile's existing fitness level and goal events. It is separate from the tour's experience answer and from an organization's rank system.

Choose the level that describes your current training. You can save a level alone. To add a target, provide a race or goal name and a valid date that is today or later. A date without a name, or a race without a date, produces a field error before anything is saved. For example, **First ultra, 50 km** with **5 June 2027** describes a target; it does not register you for that event or buy a plan.

Saving an unchanged level does not create another record. An existing goal with the same name and day is not added again. If the profile update succeeds but adding the goal fails, the screen reports that failure and retains the successful part for a retry. Continue only after checking the result. The first-run continuation opens the product's plan choices; replaying the tour from Settings does not force this follow-up again.

## After onboarding

In the current LSU iPhone build, the bottom tabs are **Today**, **Week**, **Exercises**, **Backyard** and **Me**. **Health** is a top-bar shortcut. **Progress** and **Workouts** are in the More menu; Store and Calendar have their own top controls. The web and installed app can arrange navigation differently.
